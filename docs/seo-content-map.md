## Internal linking

- The header links to Services, About, Contact, and the primary CTA. The footer links to all eight services.
- Homepage: hero questions link to four services, each challenge links to its related services, and the full service index links to all eight.
- Services overview: a question-to-service finder plus grouped listings, so every service is linked twice with descriptive anchor text.
- Each service page has breadcrumbs (Home / Services / Service), three related-service links, and a CTA that pre-selects the service on the contact form.

No ratings, reviews, address, founding date, phone, or `sameAs`. Add `sameAs` only for verified official profiles, and `address` only if the owner wants a public business address.

## Guidance for future content

- Write for people first. Each page should answer a distinct question. Don't create city pages or near-duplicate service variants.
- A blog or resources section is worth adding only when there are real articles to publish. No placeholder posts.
- Mention nationwide service where it's natural (homepage, About, FAQ). Don't repeat it in every paragraph.
