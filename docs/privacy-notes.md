It hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer and must not be published as-is. No privacy policy page is published on the site.

## If the contact form is enabled

The form sends **name, work email, company (optional), area of interest (optional), and message** directly from the visitor's browser to the configured provider (for example Formspree), which forwards them to the BNX 24 mailbox. The provider stores submissions under its own terms and may process data outside the visitor's state or country.

Before enabling it, the owner should:

1. Choose the provider and review its data processing terms, storage location, retention settings, and sub-processors.
2. Turn on spam protection at the provider, and decide whether a CAPTCHA (which may involve another third party) is acceptable.
3. Publish a privacy policy page and link to it from the form and the footer.
4. Decide on a retention period for inquiries in the provider dashboard and in the mailbox.
5. Check which state privacy laws may apply. Many U.S. state laws have revenue or data-volume thresholds, and California's CCPA/CPRA is the most commonly referenced. A lawyer should confirm what applies.
